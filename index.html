China Medical Guide — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026  |  Applies to: China Medical Guide (iOS)

1. Overview

China Medical Guide is a reading and information app that introduces international patients to hospitals and medical care in Guangzhou, China. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: the app does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you, and keeps your saved articles and notes on your own device.

2. Information we collect

Contact form. If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and a description of your needs. This information is sent to our team's email inbox so that we can reply to you. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and, if you proceed, to coordinate medical navigation services. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Content server requests. The app downloads articles from our content hosting. Like any web hosting provider, the host may process standard technical data (such as IP address) in routine server logs. We do not receive or store personal profiles from these logs.

3. What stays on your device

Your saved articles, your notes on saved articles, and reading preferences (font size, dark mode) are stored locally in the app's storage on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers. You can remove them at any time by deleting the app.

4. Permissions

The app does not request camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, or Bluetooth permissions. If you tap "Email us directly," the app opens your device's own mail app, which operates under your control.

5. Third-party services

The contact form may be delivered through a form-processing service (such as Formspree) that forwards submissions to our email inbox; that service processes the submitted fields only to deliver the message. We do not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking components, and we do not sell user data.

6. Sharing with hospitals

Only if you ask us to arrange care: with your consent, we may share your name, contact details, and medical information you choose to provide with the hospital or physicians you select, solely for appointment coordination. We do not require medical records; any medical information you choose to send is treated as confidential and is deleted within 30 days after our services end.

7. Data retention and deletion

Form submissions remain in our email inbox for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and for ordinary business records. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing us; we will delete it promptly. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including California under the CCPA/CPRA and Hong Kong under the PDPO), you may have the right to know what information we hold about you and to request its deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us by email.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy: 18928806@qq.com

China Medical Guide — 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月7日  |  适用于：China Medical Guide（iOS 版）

一、概述

China Medical Guide 是一款向国际患者介绍广州医疗资源的阅读类应用。本政策说明应用处理哪些数据、如何处理。简要版：应用不要求注册账号，不含统计 SDK 和广告 SDK，不做用户追踪，您的收藏和备注只保存在您自己的设备上。

二、我们收集的信息

联系表单。如您使用联系表单，您将提供姓名、电子邮箱及需求描述。该信息会发送至我们团队的邮箱，用于回复您的咨询；如您继续委托，用于就医导航服务的协调。我们不会将您加入任何邮件列表。

内容服务器请求。应用会从我们的内容托管服务器下载文章。与所有网站托管商一样，托管方可能在常规服务器日志中处理 IP 地址等技术数据。我们不会从日志中获取或存储个人画像。

三、仅保存在您设备上的数据

您收藏的文章、收藏备注、阅读偏好（字号、深色模式）均保存在应用在本机的存储空间中，不会上传至我们的服务器。您可以随时删除应用以清除这些数据。

四、权限说明

本应用不申请相机、相册、麦克风、定位、通讯录或蓝牙权限。如您点击"直接发邮件"，应用会唤起您设备自带的邮件应用，由您自行操作。

五、第三方服务

联系表单可能通过表单投递服务（如 Formspree）转发至我们的邮箱，该服务仅为投递目的处理您提交的字段。本应用不含广告 SDK、统计 SDK 或第三方追踪组件，不出售用户数据。

六、与医院的共享

仅在您要求安排就医时：经您同意，我们可能将您的姓名、联系方式及您选择提供的医疗信息共享给您所选医院及医生，仅用于预约协调。我们不要求您提供病历；您自愿提供的医疗信息将严格保密，并在服务结束后 30 天内删除。

七、数据保留与删除

表单提交内容将在处理咨询及日常业务记录所需期间保留于我们的邮箱。您可以随时发邮件要求删除您的信息，我们将及时处理。设备上的数据在您删除应用时即被移除。

八、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

九、您的权利

根据您所在地区的法律（包括加州 CCPA/CPRA、香港《个人资料（隐私）条例》），您可能有权了解我们持有您的哪些信息并要求删除。行使上述权利请联系我们。

十、政策更新

如本政策发生重要变化，我们将在此页面公布更新版本及新的更新日期。

十一、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：18928806@qq.com
